# Product Brief: AI Evaluation Workbench

## One-line summary

A lightweight workbench that helps AI product teams evaluate whether a GenAI feature is ready to launch.

## Problem

AI product demos often look strong in ideal scenarios but fail in edge cases. Product teams need a simple, transparent way to define quality, evaluate model behavior, and decide whether an AI feature is ready for rollout.

## Target users

- AI Product Managers
- Engineering leads
- Data science and applied AI teams
- Trust, safety, privacy, and legal reviewers
- Customer experience leaders

## Jobs to be done

When I am preparing to launch an AI-powered product experience, I want to evaluate model responses against clear product criteria so that I can make a confident launch, iterate, or no-go decision.

## MVP scope

The MVP includes:

- Synthetic evaluation cases
- Rubric-based scoring
- Failure taxonomy
- Launch decision memo
- Basic dashboard

## Non-goals

- This is not a production evaluation system
- This does not call live models
- This does not use private company data
- This does not automate final launch approval

## Product value

This workbench turns subjective AI quality discussions into more concrete product conversations about correctness, grounding, policy compliance, escalation, helpfulness, safety, and launch risk.
